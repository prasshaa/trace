# Trace decision register

Decisions that constrain how Trace is built. **Resolved** decisions are binding until a later entry supersedes them.
**Proposed** decisions are pending approval and must not be acted on.

The master brief, together with the additional product and UX requirements (received 2026-11-15), is the product
specification. This register records how its open questions were resolved.

## Standing rules from earlier phases (still in force)

- Real-resume uploads stay off unless an environment sets `TRACE_REAL_RESUME_UPLOADS` deliberately (D52 cleared that gate for the production site only). Real-data ingestion is not activated by this work.
- Hosted Supabase exists for production. It is never reset or cleaned, and records are never deleted to make tests pass. Migrations in this repository are files until someone applies them to a chosen database. Local development uses the local stack only.
- Synthetic, private-user and consented-real data are never mixed. The 18 participant resumes are not present and are
  never fabricated.
- Secrets are never printed or committed. Nothing is committed or pushed without explicit instruction.
- Every migration is additive and non-destructive, with a rollback script and pgTAP tests.
- Consent, sharing and retention policy are never changed silently.

## Resolved (2026-11-16, Phase 3.1 scope and Phase 4 decisions)

**Phase 3.1 approved.** Four new responsibility areas: AI and machine-learning product work; Data quality and governance;
Business cases and economics; Communicating with senior leadership. Existing areas are reused for L1
(`strategy_portfolio`), L2 (`people_leadership`) and L4 (`delivery_execution`), with evidence-specific conditions:
C4 transfers from business cases only with evidence of defining, measuring or tracking outcomes; L1 needs several
products or initiatives, or a complex or high-risk product; L2 needs coaching, mentoring or product leadership; L4
needs cross-team planning, dependencies and sequencing. The conservative Gap rule stays. "None of these areas" is a
user choice. Classifications record the taxonomy version; older classifications and "None" choices must be reviewed
again before they can support a Gap involving new or changed areas. No automatic Gaps, no silent re-labelling. Pack
`pm-v1.1` approved (same sources; mapping only).

**Phase 4 logic (approved).** Direct routes are always evaluated. An intermediary can be primary when it closes
material gaps that the bridge develops, the user can enter the bridge, the direct route is less supported, and the
route leads to the target. There is no rigid C1/C2/C4-only rule. The number of transitions is a trade-off, not a score.
Suitability, evidence breadth and eligibility stay separate. Unknowns are explained, never replaced by an invented
primary route.

**Phase 4 implementation (2026-11-16, awaiting review).** The curated content is in the reviewed JSON files
`pm-bridges-v1`, `pm-actions-v1` and `pm-eligibility-v1` (migration v2.22.0). The write path is
`trace_plan.record_transition_plan` (v2.23.0). The rules are in `backend/docs/TRANSITION_PLAN.md`. Two
provisional thresholds need the owner's review:
- direct support is "limited" when Gaps cover half or more of the core requirements;
- a bridge qualifies as primary when it closes at least half of the user's Gaps.
